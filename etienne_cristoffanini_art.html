2. Hosting and Server Log Files

This website is a static website and does not use user accounts, analytics tools, or profiling.

When you visit this website, the hosting provider automatically collects information transmitted by your browser. This data is necessary to ensure the security and proper operation of the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

If you contact us via the provided email address, the personal data you submit (such as your name, email address, and message) will be processed solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry or discussing a tattoo booking.

The legal basis for this processing is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to inquiries).

Your data will not be shared with third parties unless required by law.
